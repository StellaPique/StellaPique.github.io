Polaris!

Polaris is the second cycle of Project Stella Pique. It is, as all prior and upcoming iterations and bases, focused on self improvement. Since I have recently turned 18 years old as of kindling Polaris' flame, I have decided to increase the stakes drastically. STARR represents everything that Project Mayhem already represented, that every other self improvement focused work was representing during its reign, with an added bit of what Project Starlight should have become. Don't get me wrong, changing up how I dress and how I interact with people was pretty okay, but I'm ready to be the Star I want to be. I'm ready to experience life as I should have been experiencing it all this years. And I know it is impossible to make up for the time lost, for the time taken away from me, but I will not waste another second being the person I was, being the person I am still forced to be at the beginning of Polaris.

I am not immature. I haven't been immature for a year, or two. At the age of 16 I reached about most of the capacity of my intelligence I will reach, I believe. There's not much else development for me to do, only the gain of experience. The only way I can continue to improve and go forward is to embrace the person I am becoming and to honour all of my past and future sacrifices. In other words: Live life.

## Timeline

I want every important thing for the rest of my life to be a memory, or documented somewhere. I do not want to treat myself like a patient, scribbling down every piece of unimportant info or tracking every aspect about myself meticulously. I want to give every part of my life a name to remember them by, and things to remember them by. Our time is precious. Why waste good memories by forgetting?

##### Phase 01: Blonde's Heart

The first phase or whatever on the timeline. Blonde's Heart will be about setting things up and continuing my life despite the "loss" of Blondie and everyone else I thought of as dear to me. No matter how difficult or incredibly lonely things can get, I will not allow myself to stoop lower than the throne of optimism I sit on.
