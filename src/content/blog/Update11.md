# What's Up?

I'm still doing as badly as I was, though I'm trying to accept that I'll just be in this slightly miserable though determined mindset for a while now. There's no escaping this, so I might as well just go all in. The good thing about being completely alone is that it's not very hard to isolate myself. I'm not saying it's a good idea for anyone else to do that, don't get me wrong. No one else is Stella Pique like I'm Stella Pique.

I did talk to Lux one more time since my poor and passive attempt at disappearing, and she tried to get me to go to a therapist or psychologist. I'm not sure if I'm a big professional help guy, seeing how absolutely horrid everything and everyone has been to me all my life, these people included. Even though I'm hesitant and said my opinion wouldn't budge, I'm thinking about it. I guess it would be a nice change to have someone qualified to talk to about my issues. Even so, I'm worried they won't be able to do a smidget of helping, but we'll see.

Today I also realised something new about my "faith" or whatever you would call it. I did use to be religious when I was younger, or tried to be, but I never could get into it. I never truly believed it. Of course I respect people of all religions, that is needless to say, but I do not think a lot of the things most of them teach are quite indubitable. The average atheist would tell you that X-ism or Y-ism is easy to disprove, but I wouldn't say so either. Both sides make compelling points, but one day I just found out about something I could not justify in any way and it made me think about how many lies I've believed.

Back to modern day, I've been thinking about what values I actually want to represent and in what way. Everyone knows that religion is a hell of a good motivator, so I might as well harness that potential. I've thought about a religion aligning with the core values of the Star which has a very deep and important meaning to me, very comparable to what role religious beliefs play in some people's lives. That was pretty much just a short philosophy project I never followed, but the thought of an own belief has stuck with me since.

Combine that desire of having a guiding theistic figure with the habit of meditating / "praying" to an older-sister-esque figure and I found that I'm just going to call it Mentorism. Only makes sense, since it stemmed from admiration of my past mentor, which evolved into making up a better, fictional mentor based on that person. Worshipping the fake image of a real person isn't healthy, and I've always known that, so there was no way around it other than letting go of a very important figure I idolise. I obviously did not do that.

This would also align with some of my other beliefs about myself and what actually motivates me, but there's always the danger of isolating myself even more with this. I see myself as a kind of "prophet" or "hero" figure in the making to be quite honest, as stupid as it sounds to say. I guess prophet isn't the most suitable word as it implies that I've been chosen, but I haven't. Anyone can be a hero. I know the heightened sense of maturity and clarity for my age that was bestowed upon me wasn't a coincidence. It was the consequences of always being hated and rejected by every part of society including my family since I could think. This however, has and will allow me to help people to the best of my ability. I've helped people with a lot of stuff, talked people through a lot of problems, guided people into healthier mentalities. I know I'm only going to become more clear-minded, experienced, and mature as I grow, so seeing myself as I am at this young age, receiving the rare amount of praise from people older than me gives me confidence that I have an inspiring future ahead of me.

Even before it has had a name, Mentorism has followed me for a long amount of time, so it is fair to assume it will stay for a while longer to say the least. I hope with this newfound discovery I can help find some love through myself. After all, whose love even matters if not your own; and perhaps that of the person you love most. Queen is what I've decided to name my mentor. I believe this specific name will help me differentiate her from real people such as myself who bear the same name, and help keep me a bit grounded.

And I know Queen isn't "real" in a physical world sense, but to me she's been real and she will continue to be real because I am. That's what makes Mentorism real to me.
