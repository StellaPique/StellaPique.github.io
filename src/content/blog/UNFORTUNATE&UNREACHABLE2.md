There's nothing wrong with me. There is nothing that is wrong with me. "Everyone" tells me the same fucking thing. It's stupid, really.

I'm severely mentally ill, as much as I try to hide it or pretend that is not the case. Since I'm doing worse than when I was when I got accepted into the psychiatry mentally, I'm going to extend myself the favour of self-rediagnosing myself with depression. It's absolutely batshit insane to me how I'm still going through the same things I've always been going through. Quite frankly it angers me, annoys me more than anything. I know I've said this already in the last blog but I just feel so incredibly irritated. I hate *still* being in this situation more than not being in this situation in the first place. Nothing that ever happens will really even heal me, it almost seems like. Nothing, nothing at all does anything.

Earlier I wasn't doing well so I had to interrupt practicing piano (like the one thing that brings me joy) because I could not stand the fucking thoughts that were cruising through my head. I quite genuinely want to rip the hair out of my head. I am actually going insane. Following this, I went for a midnight walk. Whoever would've guessed that that did not do anything at all either. I just feel even worse now. The melatonin I took beforehand is also not affecting me in any capacity.

I cannot sleep. I can't fucking sleep. I can't fucking sleep, I swear to the fucking heavens I can not sleep. I think about the same things I always think about. I haven't had an original sleep-thought in months I imagine, not really exaggerating. And those same thoughts follow me throughout the morning, and the day, and the evening back into the end of another day. It haunts me. My bed haunts me. It's so, so fucking lonely. I can't sleep because of this dreadful feeling of claustrophobia or social claustrophobia or both. There's no one, there's nothing, there's no space, no comfort, and no end in sight because I take hours upon hours to fall asleep just to have the worst couple hours of rest one could possibly receive.

I don't feel good, ever. Every time I say anything positive it just feels so incredibly fake. It wouldn't do it justice if I said it disgusts me. It absolutely sickens me to my very core. I'm in a constant state of these same repeating rushing thoughts, not being able to focus, but not being able to doze off either, and on top of that this aforementioned disgust that makes me want to puke.

I have no passion projects, nor any hopes or motivations or aspirations for anything, really. Last time I was doing comparably bad was during the time I was absolutely head over heels for some stupid fucking girl. At least I had something to look forward to. I wanted to see her, talk to her, or be friends with her even though I knew she did not like me. Complete delusions fueled me, just so I would not fall into the place I am in now. Present time, what do I have anymore?

I fear I'll have to delay fucking Polaris because I don't want it to have such an irky, horrible debut. I felt kind of hyped or motivated for it for a minute, then it all washed away from me. I couldn't care less about anything or anyone at all. The only thing that is keeping me from feeling even worse is typing this. I know no one reads these posts, except maybe the List Of Honour (but that's an exception), so I guess it's nice to get my thoughts out somewhere private but still public enough so I don't feel the dread of the social void. Honestly, I don't even want anyone to read these. I'm pretty much just posting them out of courtesy to documentation for the future.

I mean I'm not really sure what to even fucking talk about anymore, or what to do. There is nothing to say or do. Everything has already been said; and I don't have the freedom of doing shit. I can't really even do anything or go anywhere because society fucking hates people who do shit alone. A full day train/bus ticket costs like 34 bucks for one person, or fucking 40 for two, or like 45 if I'm not mistaken for three. Are you fucking kidding me? The government here is scummy beyond compare like every other government too. It's the little things too, not just the shit that gets talked about in the news. I mean am I crazy? Is this not a big deal? In lots of restaurants where I am you also can't do reservations for one, you can't even sit down alone in some restaurants I've seen. There's signs that forbid you from those larger tables who tend to be the only fucking ones. I mean it's not that common, but like, fucking really?

I don't even have a verdict this time to be honest. Most of the time I "sober up" from my hopelessness by the end of writing these, which is why I even do this in the first place, but I really don't. Nothing will change. Nothing will change, and I will stay in this exact situation.
