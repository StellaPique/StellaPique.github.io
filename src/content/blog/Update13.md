# What's Up?

It's the day directly after my 18th birthday as of writing this. A couple incredibly interesting changes and fulfillments have happened over the last couple of days, and more will only follow.

### The Curse

To begin, I've been freed of the curse, and for good this time. The curse that has plagued me my entire life, the fear of falling behind, of not being normal. The fear of reaching the age of 18 before ever being a part of a normal friend group that I admire being around.

It was a very simple task that absolved me, but a very meaningful one. Unpromptedly, on the morning of my birthday, both people in the Blondie-Group wished me a happy birthday. I didn't even know they knew, but that's just incredibly nice. Of course more people congratulated me, but what made it special is that they are people I've always respected a fair amount. Even though I may still feel like I'm falling behind, I have ultimately fulfilled a prophecy I did not mean to fulfill.

A couple months ago I gave up on trying to force myself to be normal, and just chilled with people I liked and didn't like for the fun of it, though still trying to be as open as I can to everyone. It was ironically exactly that, which made it possible for me to stop worrying about everything so much, which gave me a way out. Splendid.

### Myself

I've decided to rename myself Stella on my 18th birthday to everyone. Before this, most people I knew in person knew me by a different name. Additionally, I've gotten my star tattooed on the back of my right hand. In a year, my left will follow. Both of these changes, I believe, will make me more of myself. Perhaps it may seem irresponsible, but in the long run it will most definitely have been an excellent choice to get a tattoo on the first day of legal adulthood. The stigma around tattoos in jobs is fading, which is really the only real reason for myself that could even scratch at my desire for expression. I know I will not be unhappy with the tattoo because the star is not only my personal emblem that symbolises everything I stand for, it *is* me.

### The Supernova

The first part of The Supernova has officially gone by, namely my birthday, exactly 10 days before the second and finish part. With this, planning has started for the project after Project Stella Pique. (Disclaimer, I'm going to say the word "Project" like a thousand times in the next couple paragraphs, but stay with me here). Obviously Project Stella Pique cannot end in the sense of concluding its lifetime and not appearing again, so I've added it, as well as Project Star & Project Starlight to *The Foundational Projects*.

A snippet from the new and improved official PSP Base: "The Foundational Projects are a series of projects which are going to follow me through about basically my whole life, so I am constituting a class for them today. A project being a part of [[The Foundational Projects]] means that it is an everlasting project with no clear end in sight. The goal is something so vague and general that it will follow me throughout my whole life, until death."

This means that these three Foundationals will more or less forever stay in the background, being the underlying forces of everything I will ever do. My ways of achieving a certain goal will change and evolve, and so will the little goals on my path, but my ultimate purpose will not waver, nor change. Another snippet from the Base: "I will always strive to improve as much as I can. No matter how much I may change, self-improvement will always be the center of my life. My hope will not waver. My determination, my clarity, my ambition will never waver."

With that said, their end in the foreground is in eight more days, as Project Star's clock is bound to both PSP and Starlight.

### What Happens After?

After the end of the first cycle, the second will follow, and it will have its own mascot project (just like Project Star was the face of all of PSP for most of the first cycle). I am still working on the planning of both the mascot and the other subs, but I'm making pretty great progress. In hindsight, cycle one did have a couple glaring weaknesses despite its tremendous success. Horrible work distribution across the time phases, lack of time phases at all after only a mere fourth of the project, and the fact that Starlight was almost useless for most of its lifetime are some reasons.

By the time I reach the age of twenty years old, which gives me two full years, I want to have won a group award in a marathon or similar physical event, released at least an album, improved myself physically, gotten a job and driver's license, and most of all I want to have succeeded in and evolved in TSPE. Beyond this, I have an extensive list of goals I've been collecting and refining on a regular which I also want to reach but will not share here, at least not just yet. Perhaps I will increase the time and with that also the goals, and perhaps my next cycle will not have a countdown at all. We will see. The only guarantee I have is that in the end, I will win.
