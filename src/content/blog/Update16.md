## Atlas: Days 1 To 7

I have visited a river, the longest castle in the world, Kufstein, the castle there, walked for hours along the austrian border for no reason at all, visited Partnachklamm and Partnachalm (where I also climbed), took a locomotive, a ferry, several busses and trains. I have tried busy-ing myself these last few days to say the least, and the pace will not stop, nor drop, but it will roll. First Traveler's Odyssey has earned five cosmic needles so far, but that will increase drastically soon I believe. On the 17th I have my first half-marathon. I've done some training, but not a lot as expected from a beginner. I do however expect myself to try my absolute best, place well, and finish in under 2:45.

Since I am going to croatia tomorrow, I'll have to train there, and I also won't have access to my pc to post blogs, check the Base, or produce music. I'll have to keep myself occupied in some way or another, so in a way I am very lucky that the half marathon is the day after my return, give or take. Though it is definitely very impractical that on the Supernova exactly I have started having huge issues with my WiFi, only exaggerated by having to go to croatia, the summer heat, and a lot of other factors trying to drag me down.

I plan to visit some bars around to see local musicians play, maybe talk to people at shared tables. Catching a couple festivals and similar also wouldn't be wrong. Atlas has only started and its boundless potential lies in my hands to discover. I'll try to keep Via Stellarum updated more or less on these issues.
