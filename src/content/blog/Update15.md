# What's Up?

Went to some kind of festival today, felt awkward, met no one at all, bought myself an overpriced glass of wine, then went home sad and empty. It's kind of ironic. This whole incident is almost symbolic for the time ahead of me if I don't change something about my life, quick & soon.

I didn't come here to whine, but to report objectively about my current situation, but if I went on with how I feel it wouldn't be distinguishable. I have conflicted feelings towards a lot of things, and despite being lonely yet again for the millionth time in my life, I'm still trying to get out of this. I've exhausted every force of power I had. There is one thing left, and I know for certain that this is the one thing that saves me, despite everything else failing. 

How I know? Hope. Pure fucking hope. The blood flowing through my veins isn't red, it's several shades of gold and an ethereal purple. There is nothing I wouldn't do to get to my goal, whatever my goal is at the time. Right now, the goal is to find some friends and to stop being so lonely. Some people strive for greatness, and land on mediocre. Others strive for perfection and land on excellence. I don't know which I would call myself right now, but one day there won't be a smidget of a doubt that I'm the latter.

To honour the immense success that it has brought me along the way, STARR will be the next mascot, at least for the foreseeable future. In addition, from now on there will be recognised cycle specific projects. To simplify, this means that each cycle will have more identity than just being another cycle of Project SP.

Cycle 2's name will be Polaris. I'm going to invest a lot of time, money, soul, and willpower into kindling Polaris' light once again. I am hopeful about this, so let's see what I can do.
