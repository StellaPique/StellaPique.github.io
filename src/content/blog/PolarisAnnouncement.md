Among the countless stars in our night sky sits one almost directly above the Earth's rotational axis. For centuries, it has been used for navigation. For centuries, it has been a clear constant for travellers. While the rest of the stars seem to rotate around it, dancing around the big black ink, Polaris hardly moves. 

It is not remarkable because it shines the brightest. It is because it is the one constant that has led people for the last 1600 years. Around 500 AD, it aligned as our North Star, and it has anchored our skies ever since.

Though with all its glory, and with all its greatness, it is not at its peak. For most of us, it will not be at its peak, it will not be the closest to north it will ever be in our lifetimes. Its potential is not infinite, but for us, it might as well be.

We are not bound by our potential, but by what we believe our potential to be. We would sooner place our trust in constants and in people more competent than us to take matters into our own hands, but it doesn't have to be this way, and countless people have shown me this. Once in a while, someone comes along with the courage to reject what others believe to be the limits of human potential. They will try a feat deemed impossible, and they will succeed.

This person, however, is not rare. This person is each and every single one of us. As you open your eyes to the beauty of life and to the beauty of trying, of putting in effort, you will realise you are that person.

For some people, being that person means striving for their dream job. For some people, it means building a family. For some people, it might be becoming famous, or it might be helping people. It could be discovering the wonders of this world, and it could be living life on their own terms, rather than what society deems normal. For lots, it might mean multiple of these, or other things. The best thing about humanity is that you have the freedom to choose for yourself.

For myself, StellaLou Pique, it means living my life to the fullest. It means squeezing every last drop of time out of my mortality. My desire to self-improve has always been rooted in the hope that one day I will stop improving simply to fill the passing hours while waiting for life to begin.

As I become older, and as I gain experience in life, my eyes have been opened time and time again to new things and ideas. Project Stella Pique, the first cycle, and the one before Polaris, has been a lot about blind improvement, as I have not had many chances to discover and achieve new things. With its passing, I want to take a moment to honour and to celebrate everything that it has done for me. I have effectively been this person from time to time, doing things I thought I would never be able to. It is not because my potential somehow increased. It is because I continued despite uncertainty, kept working, and refused to stop.

This is what makes me The Star. It is not my name, it is not the symbol I wear on my necklace, my hand, or my jewellery. It also isn't the original Stella, nor the projects that have made me who I am. It is myself, The Star, who has built myself from the ground up. The projects are a reflection of my soul, my dedication, and my willingness to improve and keep improving without ever seeing even a shimmer of light in the future.

With hopes of a new beginning, a new life, a rebirth, the light of Polaris will be set ablaze once again. The beacon of hope that leads us not only where we want to go, but where we need to go will live again inside of me. Just like the countless other beacons in this vast world of nearly 8 billion people, I will follow my path with the confidence that I am myself.

And the path that I choose to follow is hope, love, and the inspiration thereof in others. The light of Polaris will breathe again.

# My Way Forward

My way forward will be with Project Stella Pique: Polaris. In this cycle, I will try to change my approach to self-improvement from the formula I've been trying and refining for so long. The main / mascot project will be one focused on maintaining my hobbies, friendships, getting a job, continuing my education and so on. That pattern has been established. Project Mayhem did that, Project Ascend, Project Syndicate, Project Mayhem II did that too, and the latest example was Project Star.

What is new however, will be the other aspect that STARR will bring, as well as the entirety of Atlas. To begin with, STARR will not only play the role of the mascot, but will include elements similar to Starlight, though improved through the cooperation with Atlas. To put it any less vaguely, self-expression through wearing unique things, putting far more emphasis on becoming a musician, and trying to be the Stella I want to be will be a couple important points. Earning a reputation, helping others and my environment as well as generally trying to be the best role model I could be for anyone of any age is also something I want to put more emphasis on.

I think for a majority of all of my other projects I have been mostly trying to head into the "optimal" path, consistently forgetting to ask myself what *my* optimal path is.

Atlas can be described as such: "The Atlas is a pin on the map, or a thousand. The only thing it can do is show you the way, but you must walk that path alone", a cut-out from my improvement base. The Atlas will provide me with quests to do as one would find in a game, or a bucket list, or something of that caliber. The idea isn't new. Back in 2024 I got the idea to start a community project called "Project SideQuest" where we do random fun things together, collecting shared memories and such. The differences are in the fact that Atlas is something I do solo, and that the scope of the quests will be highly increased.

As soon as I can, I want to run and perhaps win a marathon of any scope, finish Camino Frances, and I want to record multiple seasons of TSPE doing that, and other things. I will challenge myself socially, physically, and in terms of discipline; and while it will demand time and money, I'm ready to handle both.

Trying new things will also be encouraged, and seeing as I am expecting to finally have an income after 18 years of being alive, I will finally have the funds to finance Cinematography, Photography, and getting into electronics.

Polaris is no longer an idea. It is a direction. Amaranthine shards break, and a warm haze is released. The cosmic bonfire has been lit.
