# What's Up?

Nothing is up, quite honestly. I'm writing this one purely out of boredom, rage, and the fear of not having a purpose. These last few days have been really, really bad. Days, or weeks, or months, or maybe even years, whatever you want to call it. Everything that has happened in my life up until now has led me to the situation in which I'm trapped now, and the person I'm trapped as. I don't want to get too poetic or whiny, but it just ain't great.

A general sense of anxiety throughout the whole day was one of my problems when I was at rock-bottom. I didn't want to admit it until now, but it's been back for a little while and it's worse this time. The only difference is that I'm not a danger to myself anymore, just extremely pissed at everything throughout the whole day (and night). In fact, I couldn't fall asleep last night because of it. I know I keep repeating this, but I felt so incredibly angry and full of hate, full of frustration. Full of fear, too. I laid in bed for two hours before deciding to do a pathetically little amount of pushups lmao, but I guess it did distract me successfully and it was the most productive thing I could have done. After a couple attempts at meditation following the short-lived half-baked "workout" I then spent a couple hours trying to fall asleep.

I think the worst thing about all of this is the lack of direction in every way imaginable. I feel like I'm genuinely about to go insane at every moment, but I don't know what I'm angry at, or what I'm afraid of. There's just nothing really left. I don't feel like myself when I'm in isolation, so there isn't really much to feel or much to look forward to. I'm just in this weird phase of existing for 14 hours a day when I'm not sleeping. I feel like I need to do something productive to calm myself down, but I don't know what to do. There's a limit to how much you can tidy a room, or how much stuff you can cook or bake to soothe your nerves, or how much you can practice piano in a day, or how much you can write. For all intents and purposes, this is in no way better than a prison. Isolating, lacking any goal or direction, and everlasting.

The Supernova was supposed to be a big, great thing. The end of Project SP, a 438-day-long compass. But it is absolutely not. I don't have much to show for it other than having gotten slightly better in a couple skills and being slightly more social. Considering the fact that this is all I've been doing and building, it's quite pathetic.

Needless to mention the elephant in the room, TSPE, my social project. Not only have I lost any and all social momentum, I've also been rid of any shimmer of hope or determination that was guiding me. Confident I don't feel either. I really don't know how I'll pull this off to be honest. On the one hand, it'll require something *beyond* what could be described as a miracle, but on the other hand I have made miracles happen before.

In a way, the more motivation I gain for rescuing TSPE, the more I lose. I shouldn't have to make miracles happen, I shouldn't have to have the emotional maturity of a vampire, and I shouldn't have to do all of this completely by myself, but I keep doing it, and I keep succeeding. I don't say this to boast, but I am genuinely some kind of boy wonder, or at least have the potential to be. Consistently getting the shortest end of the stick in every point in life is what has kept me from becoming the person I could have become, and I find that absolutely obnoxious.

I'm not saying I'm better than everyone, but I *am* saying I am better than a lot of people. I'm tired of acting like every person deserves equal admiration. Some people genuinely choose to make the world worse, and I refuse to pretend they are on the same level as someone who is doing the opposite. I'm not on some weird homelander-ish ego trip, but seeing so many people waste their lives away with alcohol, drugs, or just plain out lack of ambition pisses me off. Seeing someone waste their life away being an asshole instead of trying to help others ticks me off even more. I'm doing everything I can to get out of my hole, and from my point of view they are already more than halfway there, but guess who's going to reach their goals first. It won't be my fucking peers, I can tell you that for sure.

I feel sick to my very core at everything that happens to me, and everyone that I talk to. Getting semi-drunk that one time probably did creep out a person I admire highly, as well as a huge other group of people I still care about. Being too busy with constantly battling the feeling of an anxiety attack that never comes but only swells up in rage from time to time fucked over my chance with a couple other people. The only two reasons I'm not more distraught about those specifically is that everything is gone now either way, and that I would've stayed an outsider whatever had happened to begin with.

When I started talking about this so called "curse" of mine, it did feel like just a metaphoric overexaggeration about how difficult some things have been for me throughout my life. The more I suffer from it though, the more it actually feels like a curse. This amount of not bad, but horrible luck, time after time after time after time after time after time again can not be real. I feel pure hatred for whoever or whatever did this to me.
