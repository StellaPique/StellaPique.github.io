As you might know by me hyping up its end dramatically, the first cycle of Project Stella Pique is ending. Almost 438 days have passed by since the beginning of Project Star which started this whole thing, and in that time I have gone through a lot of changes and improvements.

What I am here for however, is to talk about you! The people that I have met in the last 438~ days which have inspired me, helped me, or supported me in any way. You showed me kindness, gave me motivation, or were a person cool enough to stand out to be in the list of honour.

I am writing this alone obviously, so do keep in mind that if you are not mentioned it doesn't mean you aren't a part of the list, it might just mean I forgot. There's a million people who inspire me, so it's hard to remember them all, and a recency bias to who is mentioned will be apparent.
