In this post I'm going over the things that have happened in the last 20 days. Today's the 60th day of Polaris. I'm presenting as Blonde Boy for the phase "A Star & No Butterfly" with the phase index of 2.

I'm not gonna lie, things have not been going as well for me as I expect, but that doesn't mean I haven't been putting in a lot of effort and very consistently at that. Pretty much daily piano practice, forcing myself in social situations I am afraid of on a daily, trying new things, trying difficult things, and so on.

Regarding the music thing: It's going in a great direction. I haven't yet found my "style" of music I want to actually publish for people to listen to and for me to become known for, but I am getting quite good at improvisation and the fact that I've been looking at the little things much more critically has opened lots of doors for me in the realm of improvement. I'm getting much, much better at making drums myself and I don't really know when I last used an awful sample instead of taking my time to make something good that actually fits for each track. I used to never do that. I've also been integrating Bass more, since I've definitely been underestimating its usefulness. It's actually astounding how many things you don't realise go into the art you consume every single day.

The social challenging part is also going quite efficiently. When I say I am not doing too well, this is the part that I mean trips me up most. Even though I'm consistently reaching out to people I've had beef with, talking to people I haven't talked to in a while, accepting every conversation with a smile on my face, it's not really working for me in the way I want it to work. I guess I am kind of an odd person in this aspect, but I really do want to be *known*, and I don't mean I want people to know my name as some mysterious cool guy. I want to be known as a role model for everyone, I want to be known as a really social person who anyone can talk to whenever they like, and I want to still be known as someone who is taken seriously.

I pretty much only started school three weeks ago so it is definitely understandable that I'm not known across the whole school in this absolutely monumental way but it does kind of sting. I don't know, I guess I have high expectations but I haven't been very satisfied with the way that my passions have been going lately and I just really need a win. A big, big win.

I'm well aware that no one reads these but if anyone who is impressionable is, do keep in mind I don't have a good mindset. I'm going to pretty much just say that outright. Yearning for something absolutely impossible, a level of fame that no one I have ever met has had before (there have been a couple, but still) is not great if you beat yourself up about it. The reason why I do it is because it makes me more productive and more goal oriented. If you want to be known, hell, even famous, great. But don't let it consume you or lead to hate. I do this because I can control and channel my emotions into healthy things extremely well.

Anyways, on a last note Polaris Days have been going pretty well. I am very consistently doing a full six step check every 8th, 18th and 28th each month without missing it most of the time. And if I do, I do it the next day. That part specifically has actually been absolutely amazing and probably one of the best additions to Polaris when contrasted with the blob of uniformity that was *The Ascent* (the first phase of Project Stella Pique. Polaris is the second for reference).

Note for future me, I am changing project aesthetics quite commonly as I am still figuring out the colour scheme I want for the project, a lot of the things will be inconsistent or changed as time goes on.
